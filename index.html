A320: Distance (NM) = (Altitude to lose ÷ 1000) × 3, then add 10 NM buffer.

A350: Clean, high-L/D wing retains energy much longer. Use (Altitude to lose ÷ 1000) × 4 as the quick multiplier, or (×3) + 15–20% as the alternate method — both push the effective ratio toward 3.5–4:1.

Why: Above FL300 the A350's lower air density means higher TAS/GS on a pure 3° idle path, often leaving it high and fast without speedbrakes.
